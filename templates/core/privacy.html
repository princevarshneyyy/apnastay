Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, update your profile, list a property, make a reservation, or communicate with us.

2. How We Protect Your Data

We implement industry-standard cryptographic password hashing (PBKDF2/SHA256), CSRF protection, and strict access controls to safeguard your personal credentials.

3. Data Sharing

We only share essential booking information (such as guest name, stay dates, and guest count) with your host to facilitate your stay. We do not sell user data to third parties.

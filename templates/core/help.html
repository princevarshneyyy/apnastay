Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about booking, cancellations, hosting, and payments on ApnaStay.

Search for your desired destination on the homepage or explore page. Select your check-in and check-out dates and guest count. Once you choose an available property, click "Reserve Now", review your booking summary, and confirm. Your reservation ID will be generated instantly.

For this version, ApnaStay operates on a "Pay at property upon check-in" model. You will receive a full price breakdown showing the accommodation subtotal and platform fee, and payment is settled directly with your host upon arrival.

Yes! You can cancel any upcoming booking before the check-in date directly from your Guest Dashboard ("My Trips"). Once cancelled, the dates are immediately released in our database for other travellers.

Any registered user can become a host with a single click by selecting "Become a Host" in the navigation bar. Fill out your property title, description, address, price per night, amenities, and upload property photos. Your listing will immediately be published to travellers across India.

To ensure 100% authentic feedback, only guests who have completed a stay at the property can submit a review and 1-5 star rating. Hosts cannot review their own properties, and each completed stay allows exactly one review.

About ApnaStay

"Apna Stay, Apni Choice" – Empowering authentic travel experiences across India through community-driven hospitality.

Our Mission

ApnaStay was founded with a singular purpose: to make experiential accommodation accessible, trusted, and delightfully easy across India. Whether you are seeking a beachside villa in North Goa, an apple orchard retreat in Manali, a heritage haveli in Jaipur, or a serene backwater homestay in Alleppey, ApnaStay connects conscious travellers with welcoming local hosts.

Built as a production-grade full-stack project, ApnaStay combines high-performance Django architecture, robust database design, clean RESTful APIs, and an elegant, mobile-friendly interface inspired by world-class travel marketplaces.

Pan-India Stays

Properties across metro cities, beach hubs, hill stations, and cultural destinations.

Safe & Verified

Verified host profiles, authentic guest reviews, and clear cancellation rules.

Modern Engineering

Powered by Python, Django, PostgreSQL, and responsive mobile-first UI.
